What to try: Build a clean diagonal cluster of 8–10 points, note the R². Then drag just one point way off to the side, far from the trend. Watch the line tilt toward it and R² collapse — one point can out-vote almost everyone else, because least-squares punishes distance quadratically. A point twice as far off pulls four times as hard.
